Lemon Sugar Cookies

Low sweetness. Works well with 1-for-1 gluten-free flour

✅With shortening

recipe source

The shortening makes them fall apart a bit more, as it tends to do.

150 g - sugar
50 g - brown sugar
1 u - lemon zest
2 u - egg
1 t - vanilla
1 u - lemon juice
320 g - flour🔀Mix
1 t - baking powder
0.5 t - salt
