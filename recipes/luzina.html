This variant mushes the quince before they become sticky jam.

1.125 kg - quincePrep : peel, core, chunkmush : use a food processor to make smooth🔀MixBring to a boil : on medium-high heatSimmer : on medium heat (until very thick)
0.56 kg - sugar
2 b - lemon juice
0.25 kg - water
1 t - cardamon podsgrind : in a mortar&pestle or spice grinder
