French Toast

French toast is a delicious breakfast treat. Making it in a casserole dish is easy and make-ahead, ideal for entertaining.

✅My mom's version

We make this casserole every brunch we host. It's always a big hit, takes little effort, and is made in advance; making it ideal for entertaining.

200 g - brown sugarMake caramel : Bring to a boil. Stir. Boil for 2 minutes, until brown, thick, and bubbly. Turn off heat.Spread : Spread into 9 inch * 13 inch casserole dishCover : cover with breadCover : cover with berriesCover : cover with breadPour : pour custard evenly. Cover with plastic wrap and press down slightly so the bread absorbs the custard❄ : chill in fridge for overnight🔥Bake : at 350°F / 180°C for 45
